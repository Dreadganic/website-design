Questions

When is my coffee roasted?

We roast twice a week and ship each delivery within two days of roasting, so it arrives at its best.

Can I pause, skip or cancel?

Yes, at any time before your next roast day. There is no minimum number of deliveries.

Can I change coffee or grind?

Yes. Switch coffee, grind, rhythm or number of bags for any upcoming delivery.

How does shipping work?

We ship from Osaka by courier across Japan. Shipping is shown at checkout before you pay.

Can I give a subscription as a gift?

Yes. Add the recipient's address at checkout and leave a message in the order notes.
