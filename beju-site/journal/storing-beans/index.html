How to keep your coffee fresh

At home · 3 min read · 14 October 2026

Where to keep beans, how long they last, and whether to use the freezer.

Roasted coffee is at its best for a few weeks. Oxygen, light, heat and moisture all fade the flavour, so a few simple habits make a big difference.

The short version

  • Keep beans in their bag with the valve closed, or in an airtight container.
  • Store them somewhere cool and dark, away from the stove and sunlight.
  • Avoid the fridge: coffee picks up moisture and smells there.
  • Grind just before you brew.

When to drink it

Brew methodStart drinkingBest within
Filter3–5 days after roast5 weeks
Espresso7–10 days after roast6 weeks

Freshly roasted coffee releases carbon dioxide for several days. A short rest lets it settle so it brews evenly, especially as espresso.

Can I freeze coffee?

Yes, if you won't finish it within a month. Split it into airtight portions and freeze them once. Let a portion reach room temperature before opening so no condensation forms, and don't refreeze it.
