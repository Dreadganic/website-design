Beju bag labels, 16 Oct 2026
For plain kraft valve pouches (e.g. coffee-fukuro.net 160 x 230 x 70 mm) until printed bags are ready.

print/    print-ready PDFs, one per label (send these to the sticker printer)
guides/   the same with the cut line (pink) and safe area (blue), for checking only
preview/  PNG previews

FRONT LABEL  beju_label-front_<coffee>.pdf
  100 x 125 mm trim, 2 mm bleed on every side (file is 104 x 129 mm). Suggested 3 mm rounded corners.
  Place it centred, below the degassing valve. Check the position on a real pouch first.
  Single origins say 200 g; Casablanca Blend says 250 g.

BACK LABEL (一括表示)  beju_label-back_<coffee>.pdf
  70 x 92 mm trim, 2 mm bleed (file is 74 x 96 mm). White, so it can be written on.
  Fill in by hand or stamp: 賞味期限 (best-before date) and tick the 挽き方 (grind) box.
  名称・原材料名・内容量・保存方法・製造者 follow the usual layout for roasted coffee beans under Japan's food labelling rules.
  Please confirm the wording, and your best-before period, with your local 保健所 before printing in quantity.

PRINTING
  Colour: RGB PDF with the fonts embedded. Most online sticker printers accept this (for example
  Raksul, Printpac or Sticker Mule); choose matte paper or kraft-coloured paper, and permanent adhesive.
  A first run of 50 of each front label plus 250 back labels is enough for the launch run.
