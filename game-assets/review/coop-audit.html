Co-op audit: the right amount of play, too much on screen

I checked everything built for the community side against three questions: is it asking for the right amount of co-op play, does it fit the rest of the game, and should each piece be there at all. The numbers come from the game itself, the server code, the earlier playthroughs and a test with every feature switched on. I compared it with how other idle and casual games run their co-op. Nothing has been changed yet.

The short version

~1 mina day of deliberate co-op play: a few Help taps, a sticker, a claim. Everything else counts from normal play.
11goals on the Daily tab at once for a co-op member (3 daily, 2 weekly, the co-op order, 4 Derby tasks, Harvest Day), on a 2,300 px page with 409 words and 23 buttons
+50% to +130%more cranes from play each week, against a design that keeps play cranes low so real orders are the fast lane
5 at oncenew things at level 9: Ranks, the season track, farm visits, co-ops and Yellow Bourbon
Spot on
  • How much co-op play it asks for. It's light and passive, which suits 8-minute idle sessions.
  • The themes: real coffee co-ops, Harvest Day and the Cup of Excellence all belong in a coffee game.
  • Stickers only, with no chat to moderate.
  • The Cup of Excellence as the endgame.
Too much
  • Screen space: the co-op section is longer than the rest of the Daily tab.
  • Cranes: about 22 a week from the co-op order and up to 24 from the Derby.
  • Help: seven helps cut drying time by 79%.
  • Goals that ratchet up: a co-op only hits 100% by repeating last week exactly.
Missing or wrong
  • Inactive members still add at least 1,000 kg each to the order.
  • Golden-bush Harvest Days ask for 1.5–2 hours from each player who joins in.
  • "Q grader" now means three different things.
  • New players can see Harvest Day at minute 5.
  • The Cup has no reward beyond the board.

How much co-op play

Co-op play here is mostly passive. The coffee you sell anyway fills the co-op order, your normal play scores Derby points, and Harvest Day counts what you already do. The only deliberate co-op actions are these:

ActionHow oftenTimeRead
Help a co-op mateonce a day each, up to 7~10 sThe one real co-op act. Too strong: each help cuts their drying by a fifth.
Send a stickerany time~2 sOptional and safe. It doesn't need 12 on screen.
Claim order tiersup to 4 a week~5 sFine, but four claims for one order is fiddly.
Claim the Derbyonce a week~2 sFine
Choose the week's processweeklynoneThe order rotates washed, natural and honey, so the only co-op decision is to process more of that.
Harvest DaymonthlynonePassive, plus one claim

Verdict: about right. Idle players play in short sessions, and the strongest lesson from other games (below) is that co-op features become chores when they demand coordination or daily attendance. Here, missing a day costs nothing, and nobody can be kicked or let down by a missed slot. The one thing a little thin is shared decisions: a co-op never chooses anything together. That's a fair trade for now, and the place to add it later is the weekly order, for example by letting the co-op pick between two processes.

  • Level 9 is crowded again. We spaced the start out so nothing brings more than three new things, and this puts five at level 9, a level before the prestige walk.
  • Too many goal lists in one place. A co-op member sees daily tasks, weekly tasks, the co-op order with 4 tiers, a 4-task Derby board, Harvest Day and the season track. Some overlap: in the test, "Cup 80 lots by hand" was a weekly task and a Derby task at the same time. One action counting twice is fine; showing both in full on one page isn't.
  • Names collide. "Q grader" is the cupping notebook's top title and charm, the Derby's top league, and the Cup of Excellence rating. Keep it for the Cup, where it means a certified cupper, and rename the other two.
  • Fits the game's themes well. Co-ops are how real smallholders sell. Harvest Day lines up with real launches. The Cup of Excellence is a real competition and turns the skills the game teaches (cupping, picking a process, catching the crack) into an endgame.
  • Fits the economy badly. The README says play cranes are kept low so real bejucoffee.com orders are the fast lane.

  • Ours is lighter than all of them, on purpose. They are session-heavy games where the group is the main loop. In an idle game the farm runs itself, so light, passive co-op play fits. Casual games tend to start with light social features and add team competitions later, which is the order we're in.
  • Small groups need sleeper protection. Lasting groups are usually 30–50, and small ones (Egg, Inc.) re-form every contract. At 8, one sleeper is 12.5% of a co-op, which is why inactive members should count as zero.
  • Help is always capped, and the helper is paid. Ours pays a season star and is capped, which matches. It's just stronger than anyone else's.
  • Presets aren't immune to misuse. Add a "hide stickers" switch for anyone who'd rather not see them.
  • Global goals should be built to succeed, in tiers. Harvest Day could add a halfway tier, so a quiet weekend still pays something.

Should each piece be there?

PieceCallWhy, and what to change
Farm visits and CheersKeepCheap, safe, gives cosmetics an audience. No change.
Harvest DayKeep, tuneShow it from level 5, not minute 5. Golden goals are too big: golden bushes appear every 3–6 minutes, so 8 per active player, with perhaps 40% joining in on a given weekend, means about 20 each, or 1.5–2 hours of play. Make it 3 per active player. Lower the smallest goals (lots 200, cups 100, golden 25) so it works with a handful of players at launch. Add a halfway tier of 2 cranes, so a quiet weekend still pays.
Co-ops: joining, members, invite codesKeep, moveOpen them after the first prestige (level 6 of P1) rather than at level 9, which is already crowded. Give the co-op its own page inside the Daily tab (Today · Co-op) instead of stacking it under the tasks.
Weekly co-op orderKeep, fixSize it at 80% of last week instead of 100%, so a normal week reaches the top tier. Members who added nothing last week and haven't been seen for 7 days count as zero. Make one claim that pays every tier reached, not four buttons.
Co-op cranesCut backTiers of 1, 2, 3 and 4 cranes (10 in all, the same as the old weekly order), so play cranes stay where they were.
HelpKeep, soften10% per help instead of 20%, and at most 3 a day received, so the best case is about 27% faster rather than 79%.

Nothing needs cutting outright. The Derby is the only piece built ahead of need, and it stays hidden until there are players for it.

Suggested changes

1 · Economy and fairness

Co-op tiers 1/2/3/4 cranes and one claim. The order at 80% of last week. Inactive members count as zero. Help at 10%, three a day. Derby rewards: a badge plus 1–3 cranes.

2 · Space and timing

A Co-op page inside the Daily tab, with stickers and the Derby trimmed. Co-ops at P1 level 6. Harvest Day from level 5.

3 · Names and goals

Golden and smallest Harvest goals lowered, with a halfway tier. A "hide stickers" switch. "Q grader" kept for the Cup only. A weekly badge for the Cup's top 10%.

All three are small and touch code built this week. The server tests cover the order and Help rules, so they'd be updated in the same change.

How this was checked

The test ran the real server code on an in-memory store, with 100 test farmers in 20 co-ops so the Derby switched on. One save joined a co-op during a live Harvest Day; the Daily tab was then measured and screenshotted on a 390 px phone screen. Lot, cup and golden-bush rates come from the three playthroughs to prestige 3 (the bot misses 45% of golden bushes, as a person might). Crane figures come from the game's reward tables. The comparison with other games is from published sources, listed below.
