Playtest · 18 October 2026 · three human-like runs of 120 hours, aiming for P8

Up to P5 the game is well paced; from P5 to P6 it takes about 45 hours with little to do

Three bots played for 120 hours each, about 216 sessions of 20–45 minutes with breaks of up to 18 hours: two with the new building costs, one with the old. All three reached P6. None got near P7. This report covers the building cost change, the 2.5-second crack windows and what the game is like after P5.

What changed

Building costs (×2 past level 5): a small help

P5 came about 45 minutes sooner (10.1–10.4 h against 11.1 h). P6 came at about the same time, because by then the buildings are maxed and costs no longer matter. Levels 6 and 7 are now steady purchases instead of one big save each.

Crack windows of 10 down to 2.5 seconds: the roastery's share settled

From P4 to P5 the roastery earned 20–24% of coins, down from 27–30% with the wider windows. Rare beans now often miss their crack, which is the difficulty you wanted.

Three problems
  • P6 is too far away. P6 needs level 50, and each level past 10 costs 1.22× the one before. Farm income stops growing once buildings reach the P5 cap of 7, so players gain under one level an hour for about 45 hours. P7 (level 58) would take months.
  • There's little left to buy. Across 45 hours a player makes 80–90 purchases, mostly farm mastery, with about 60 stretches of 10 minutes or more and some of over two hours where nothing new is affordable. The P1–P4 runs had a purchase every few minutes.
  • The roastery runs out too. It earns about two-thirds of income from P5 on, which is the takeover we wanted. But reputation 20 and every one of the 28 cosmetics are done by about 25 hours, halfway to P6, and after that the roastery has no new goals.

Options

1 · A softer level curve past level 30 (recommended)

Make each level past 30 cost 1.15× the last instead of 1.22×. Getting from level 42 to 50 would take about a quarter of the XP, so P5 to P6 would drop from about 45 hours to roughly 12–15. P6 to P7 would need a sixth of the XP. Levels up to 30, and so everything up to about P3, stay exactly as they are.

2 · A building cap step at P5

Add a cap of 8 at P5, between the 7 at P4 and the 9 at P6, so there's something big to save for during P5 and income keeps growing.

3 · Roastery goals beyond reputation 20

Master roaster stars: past reputation 20, every further 2,000 reputation earns a star on your roastery sign and +1% bag price, up to 10 stars. A seasonal roast "streak", making that season's recipe several times, could also count towards season stars.
