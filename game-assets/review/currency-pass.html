One currency a screen

Phase 2 · T2.7 · the anti-goal "no new currency: one per screen, island money stays on the island", measured on every screen and turned into a check that fails when a new screen breaks it.

How it was measured

sim/currency_audit.js opens all 26 tabs, sub-pages and Kona pages on a prestige 6 farm with every tab open. On each one it reads every button: a button with a number and a currency word is a price. Rewards (+60 know-how, Claim 10 cranes) don't count. Mentions in the text are listed separately, since naming a reward isn't asking you to spend. The currencies are coins, know-how, cranes, season stars and Kona's shells.

Island money stays on the island: shells appear only on Kona's pages, and no Kona page prices anything in coins, know-how or cranes. A planted coin price in the Kona shop is caught (mainland money on the island).

The three mixes, and why they stay for now

  • Farm › Fields: estate stages cost coins and know-how on one button. This is the only two-currency price in the game, and it's on purpose: estates are the late-game sink for both, and the card names both amounts.
  • Shop › Farm style: building styles for cranes, decor for coins. These are two sections, each opening with its own wallet line ("[your] cranes. Buy a style once…", "[your] coins. Buy once…"), so in practice each section has one currency.
  • Shop › Roastery: two Beju brand pieces for cranes (the Beju label, 10, and the gold noren, 20). Everything else is coins or earned.

The audit lists these three by name with their reason (MIXED), so it passes on today's game. Any other screen that adds a second price, or any shells off the island, fails it.

Your call (no change made)

  • Roastery crane pieces and shop charm. Every roastery cosmetic adds +1% to bag prices (up to +20%), so the two crane pieces are, technically, cranes buying a sliver of power. The design rule is "cranes buy identity, never power." Options: leave it (it's 2% and capped), keep crane-bought pieces out of the charm count, or price them in coins. It's an economy decision, so I haven't touched it.
  • Farm style → two sub-tabs? Splitting Decor into its own Shop tab would make it strictly one currency a screen with no price changes, at the cost of a sixth Shop tab on a phone. I'd leave it.
