Design proposal · 18 October 2026 · roastery cosmetics

Roastery cosmetics can fill the empty reputation levels and give late-game coins somewhere to go

The roastery now has its own scene, so anything a player changes there shows up straight away, like decorations on the farm. This plan covers six kinds of cosmetics, where each one comes from, and the order to build them in. Two problems get solved along the way: ten of the 19 reputation levels after the first give nothing yet, and players sit on large piles of coins between P4 and P5.

What players could change

Roaster finishes · 7 · cheap, they're colour swaps of the current drum

The drum, hopper and trim change colour. The brass band for 160 kg drums stays on every finish, so drum size still shows.

Classic redCopperBeju greenEnamel creamShowa blueSakuraMaster gold
Noren curtains · cheap

The cloth over the door. Indigo crest is the default. Beju crane, gold, and one for each monthly season (sakura, tanabata, momiji and so on), made from the season colours the same way the season hats and building styles are.

Shop props · 6 sprites

Three spots: one on the wall, one on the counter, one on the floor. The props: a record player that crackles in time with the music, a waving maneki-neko, a siphon bar, a neon COFFEE sign, a shop cat asleep on the sacks, and hanging plants.

Shop interiors · 4 · the most art

New floors and walls for the whole room: Kissaten (dark wood, green velvet booths), Osaka modern (concrete and plywood), Showa retro (checkerboard floor, tin signs) and Machiya (tatami, shoji and an ink-brush menu). Brick and tile stays the default.

Bag labels and aprons · cheap

Label colours on the bags on the shelf and in customers' hands, and aprons for your assistant and cashier (denim, Beju green, kissaten black, sakura). Real Beju labels are the crossplay version, described below.

Everything here is cosmetic. None of it changes prices, speed or quality, so the balance from the P5 playthrough stays as it is.

Where it shows

  • In the roastery scene, as soon as it's chosen, the same way building styles show on the farms.
  • A Roastery page in the Shop, next to Building styles, with Use and Buy buttons and the source of each locked item ("Reputation 13", "100 bags to kissaten", "Season track tier 12").
  • On the leaderboard card (later): your roaster finish as a small badge beside your name. The server only accepts approved cosmetics on the board, so it would need adding to that list. That's a small change.

Build order

  1. First: roaster finishes, noren (default, crane, gold and the season generator), the six props and their three spots, the reputation-level gifts and Shop › Roastery with coin prices. All of it uses small sprites and colour swaps, so it fits in one round of work.
  2. Next: buyer gifts, the season track tiers, aprons and bag labels, and perfect-crack titles.
  3. Last: the four interiors (each a full new background) and real Beju labels through cranes.

Decisions for you

  • Coin prices: should Shop › Roastery be the late-game coin sink (250,000 to 25 million coins), or should these cosmetics only be earned?
  • Season track: is it OK to swap tiers 12 and 22 from coins to the month's noren and bag label?
  • Real labels: may the game use Beju's real bag designs for crane rewards? If so, I'll need the label artwork or a description of it.
  • Leaderboard: should the roaster finish show on board cards now, or later?
