Moods and regulars

Phase 2 · T2.1 weekly buyer mood board + T2.2 regulars with memory · shipped together because the second hooks into the first.

What they fix

The deep analysis named two problems: variety and process are solved choices (there is always one optimal coffee, so the choice stops being a choice), and the counter's regulars have no memory — ten visits and a mug, then nothing. Both fixes below cost zero Netlify function traffic: the board is a pure function of the calendar, and the memories live in the save.

T2.1 · The weekly mood board

  • Each of the three buyers and the five regulars publicly fancies one coffee per week: a variety × base-process key from VAR × washed/natural/honey (27 slots), paying +15–25% coins when sold or served to the fancier.
  • Deterministic: FNV-1a over ISOweek|who|salt (UTC-anchored, moodWeek()). Same board on every device and for every co-op mate, no server, no drift. The game's own seeded rnd is never consumed.
  • Pay lands in one point, bagPrice, so it flows everywhere consistently: buyer shelf, Sell all, bestBuyer routing, and the counter (pre-orders, walk-ins, regulars). Reputation is untouched — ctRep never reads bagPrice — so pacing and prestige math are unaffected.
  • Shown as a card on the Daily tab (first view of the week fires mood_view, once per save per week) and as a one-line strip on the counter page.
  • Design intent: the +15–25% bonus only pays on an exact bean match, so the optimal roast changes every Monday. The solved choice becomes a weekly decision: what to plant, what to process, what to keep on the shelf.

T2.2 · Regulars with memory

  • A regular's order now follows this week's mood when the farm can make that coffee (ctRegWant): "Aya only wants washed this week" is literally true. Otherwise she orders a favourite from what's on hand, as before.
  • Visits (serves) now tell a story: a 3-line story at 5, 15 and 40 visits per regular — 15 stories, told by the regular in their own voice, as a modal (reg_story beacon event with reg + milestone).
  • The mug still lands at 10 visits (unchanged), and the Neighbourhood roaster title still needs all five at 10. The 40th visit gifts the Counter confidant title.
  • The Regulars strip now shows the arc: visits, next story milestone, mug.
