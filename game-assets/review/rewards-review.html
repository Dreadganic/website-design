Game review · 18 October 2026 · measured with the playtest bot

Free coffee comes too easily, and cranes don't need orders

Beju Farm's loop works: a game people return to, real orders that feed it, and real coffee as the prize. The two links to real money are out of balance, though. Free samples come in under an hour and can't be redeemed yet. Cranes from play outpace cranes from orders about ten to one.

The bot played the current build as an active player and as a casual one (three 10-minute sessions a day). It plays faster than people do, so read times as lower bounds. Sample values use Beju's 200 g prices of ¥2,800–4,500.

Findings

What the playtest showed

1 · Samples

All five free bags in under an hour

Active play earned the Mogiana, Huila, Gedeo, Lake Toba and Turrialba samples at 12, 18, 30, 30 and 48 minutes. A casual player had all five within 1.7 days. Farms open quickly and 1,000 kg is a small harvest once pickers are hired, so the goal never slows down.

2 · Redemption

Samples live only on the device

An earned sample is a line saved in the browser. Nothing on the server records it, and nothing at checkout adds it to an order. Clearing the browser earns all five again. Before samples are promised to customers, they need a server record and a clear way to redeem them.

3 · Cranes

Play gives about ten times what orders give

Daily tasks (3 a day), the weekly order (30), check-ins and level milestones added up to 100 cranes in week one and about 60 a week after. That equals ¥6,000–10,000 of orders every week. A customer buying one ¥3,000 bag a month gets 30. The "cranes only" Osaka garden opens within a week without any order, so ordering coffee barely changes the game.

4 · What works

Real coffees that change the game

Orders that unlock processes, stamps that unlock backgrounds, and cranes that only buy cosmetics are the right shape. They give orders real meaning in the game without costing Beju anything per crane. Server-side claims stop the same order being claimed twice.

Recommendations

A reward ladder people earn and redeem with orders

A · Sample ladder

One welcome bag, then one bag per prestige milestone

  • Welcome sample: Mogiana's first 1,000 kg, as now (about 12 minutes). It's the hook, and it comes with the first order.
  • Origin samples: Huila at prestige 2, Gedeo at prestige 3, Lake Toba at prestige 4, Turrialba at prestige 5. For the casual bot that's about day 1, 3, 7 and 14. For people it's likely 2–3 times longer, so roughly a month for all five.
  • Monthly sample, after the ladder: check in on 20 days of a month to earn one sample for that month. It rewards loyalty like a streak, and the cost is capped at one bag per customer per month.

Prestige is already tuned, checked by the server and paced by the level curve, so samples inherit that pacing instead of needing their own balance.

B · Redemption

Redeem with an order, checked on the server

  • One sample per order of ¥3,000 or more, chosen at checkout or added automatically.
  • The server checks the player's recorded prestige or check-ins before allowing it, so editing the save does nothing.
  • An earned sample lasts 60 days, giving a gentle reason to order and a cap on outstanding promises.

With every sample attached to a ¥3,000+ order, the cost works like a 10–15% loyalty reward on those orders at retail value, and less at cost price. Starbucks Japan gives back about 8–9%.

C · Cranes

Make orders the main source of cranes

  • Daily "sell 2 lots" task: 3 cranes down to 1. Weekly team order: 30 down to 10. Check-in streak cranes: 2 and 5 down to 1 and 3.
  • That brings play to roughly 15–20 cranes a week, so a ¥3,000 bag (30, or 50 with a new stamp) equals about two weeks of play.
  • Osaka then takes about a month of play, or one order and a week. Real orders become the fast lane, as intended.
D · Website

Close the gaps between the shop and the game

  • Thank-you page: an "Add this order to Beju Farm" button linking to /farm/?claim= with the session ID.
  • Checkout: a "Free 100 g from your farm" option when a sample is waiting.
  • Subscriptions: award cranes on each paid renewal through the Stripe webhook.
  • When you're ready to launch, link the farm from the footer and the thank-you page, and say on product pages what each coffee unlocks.
