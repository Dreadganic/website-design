Care packages and postcards

Phase 2 · T2.3 co-op care packages + T2.4 visit postcards + T2.5 seasonal-roast stars · shipped together because all three deepen ties the game already has (a co-op, a farm visit, the season track) without adding a single new screen.

What they fix

The social layer could only take: stickers, help, cheers. Nothing you make can travel to another farmer, and a farm visit ends with a single anonymous tap. Care packages let your roast carry your name to a mate's shelf; postcards let a visitor leave a thought, not just a count. Both are server-checked, capped, and free text stays out.

T2.3 · Care packages (co-op)

  • From the co-op's Members row, Send a bag opens your roasted shelf grouped by coffee + roast (best cup shown), and mails one bag to that mate. It arrives at their next check-in with your name and its cup score, priced by their farm's own saleFor math — a client-declared coin value is never trusted, and the bag lands at quality 70 with the sender's cup noted.
  • One send per farmer per day, checked server-side (day-keyed blob coops/{id}/care/{dayJST}, inbox capped at 3). Both sides earn +1 season star, once a day each — the sender on send, the receiver on first delivery. It lands in the Stars-today board like every other star.
  • Mates are addressed by list index (never ids), the delivered payload carries only name/key/profile/cup — player ids never reach other players — and day blobs read yesterday + today so a package mailed late JST still arrives.
  • No chat, by design: the bag and its cup score are the message.

T2.4 · Visit postcards

  • Six preset compliments (lovely, bloom, roast, coffee, farm, wow), each with its own sprite: "The cherries look happy", "Save me a cup", "Farm goals!". One tap on any farm visit; the single Cheers button becomes a six-card choice.
  • A postcard rides the Cheers — same handler, same limits (one per farm per day per visitor, 30 farms a day) — so there is no new rate surface to abuse. Unknown card ids are dropped server-side.
  • The host's keepsake shelf keeps the last five, shown when they view their own farm: who left it and what it said. Cosmetic only; nothing else changes.
  • postcard_send is whitelisted in the beacon handler with a 12-char pc field.

T2.5 · Seasonal-roast stars

  • The last piece of the master-roaster loop: roasting the season's recipe now earns a season star (once a day, manual roasts only — matching the daily task), on top of the existing +20% seasonal bag price and tier-8 unlock.
  • Shown in Stars-today whenever the roastery is open, so the reason to reach tier 8 is visible before you have it.
