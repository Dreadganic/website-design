Concept art · 18 October 2026 · estate projects, before any mechanics are built

Each farm's drying area at stages 1, 2 and 3, plus a seasonal showpiece

These are drawn as overlay sprites that the game can use as they are. Each row is one farm's drying area (the left two-thirds of the scene, above the fields), shown at three times its game size. The last column adds a seasonal showpiece, shown in November's Momiji colours. Everything sits below the hearts and level counters, so none of it is hidden.

Still to come in the build

  • Animation: the raker and the women at the beds move, steam rises from the dryer, the boat rocks and comes and goes, water runs in the ponds and channels, and the koi swim.
  • Showpieces: these are placeholders, a tree and a banner recoloured per season. The final versions would have a proper design for each month: kadomatsu, carp streamers, tanzaku wish strips, maple and so on.
  • Weather: rainy-season and winter versions, such as tarps on the beds and snow on Osaka's roofs.
