Design research · 18 October 2026

Prestige perks fit the genre: give one each prestige, and make them conveniences, not multipliers

Idle and farming games reward long-term players by taking chores off their hands, usually at the exact point a chore starts to grate. Beju Farm's prestige already avoids income multipliers so the season board stays fair. Free convenience perks are the natural way to make each prestige feel bigger. Cup all at prestige 2 is the first one; here is a ladder for the rest.

What other games do

GameWhat carries across resetsWhat it teaches us
Antimatter DimensionsChallenges and milestones unlock autobuyers that automate the earlier layers of the gameAutomation is the reward for doing a loop many times. Each milestone removes a chore the player has already mastered.
Cookie ClickerHeavenly upgrades and permanent upgrade slots keep chosen upgrades across ascensionsPlayers value whatever speeds the climb back after a reset.
Egg, Inc.Epic research, golden eggs and the Hyperloop survive prestige; silos extend away timeLonger away time and keeping key buildings are prized permanent rewards for casual players.
Melvor IdleMastery pool checkpoints at 10, 25, 50 and 95% unlock skill-wide bonusesClear, visible thresholds turn long grinds into goals.
Stardew ValleyAfter level 10 in all skills, XP becomes mastery points spent at the Mastery Cave for powerful perksEven a cosy farming game gives its veterans a new layer of rewards for maxed play.
Idle Miner TycoonManagers and Super Managers automate shafts; prestige adds permanent bonusesAutomation is unlocked gradually, one station at a time.

The common thread: repeat players earn their way out of repetition. The perk arrives once the chore has stopped being interesting, never before. That matches what we saw with Cup all: tapping each lot is fine with 5 racks and tedious with 15.

Why conveniences and not multipliers

  • Seasons: the board ranks capped season stars, so new and old farmers race on equal terms. An income multiplier wouldn't break that, but stacked bonuses would widen the gap between new and veteran farms everywhere else.
  • Pacing: P5 sits near 10.7 hours of active play. Multipliers per prestige would shrink every later run and make the geometric level curve meaningless.
  • Active play: perks must not do the active-play jobs (golden bushes, choosing to cup) for players who aren't there. Cup all still needs you to tap it.
  • Real money: perks stay free. Selling convenience for cranes would mean selling it for coffee orders, which feels pay-to-win.

A perk for each prestige

Timed against the playtest bot's run to P5: 3 farms in the P1 run, 4 in the P2 run, all 5 by P3; racks go 6-9, 10-17, 17-27; a run takes 25 minutes at P1 and about 8 hours at P4.

  1. P1soon
    Seed catalogue: Replant allChoose a coffee and every field on every open farm switches to it in one tap, except each farm's native field, which keeps its terroir.The P1 run brings a third farm and a new coffee. Replanting 9 to 12 fields one by one through a popup each time is the first real chore.
  2. P2built
    In the gameHead cupper's table: Cup allHand-cup every lot on the racks in one tap.Racks jump past 10 in the P2 run.
  3. P3soon
    Night shift: 18 hours awayThe farm keeps working for up to 18 hours while you're away, up from 12.By P3 the casual player is weeks in and playing around a daily routine; a night's sleep plus a workday is more than 12 hours. Egg, Inc.'s silos show how much players value this. Offline pay is lower than active play, so active players keep their lead.
  4. P4soon
    Old friends: Huila and Gedeo stay awakeAfter a prestige, Huila and Gedeo keep working straight away; only Toba and Turrialba sleep until you level back up.From P4 a run lasts hours, and the first stretch with farms asleep is the slowest part. This speeds the climb back, as Cookie Clicker's permanent slots do, without raising the ceiling.
  5. P5later
    Lookout: golden bushes on every farmGolden bushes can appear on any open farm, and the alert takes you there in one tap.With 5 farms, players spend time away from the farm on screen and miss bushes. This keeps active play rewarding, not easier.
  6. P6later
    Tasting notes: reroll a daily taskSwap one daily task a day for a new one.Late-game players often get a task their farm makes awkward. Many task-board games allow a reroll. It doesn't add stars, since the task cap stays at 6 a day.
  7. P7later
    Foreman's notebook: a process for each coffeeSet the foreman's process per coffee (Geisha always washed, Pink Bourbon always whiskey, for example) instead of one rule for everything.With every process and coffee unlocked, one foreman rule leaves money on the table, and fixing it by hand is fiddly.
  8. P8later
    Golden Crane: the farm signA gold Beju sign over every farm and a crane that flies over the fields. Cosmetic only.The top crest tier deserves something everyone can see on the farm itself, and by then there is nothing left to automate.

Each perk shows in the prestige preview and popup and in the perk list under Ranks > Prestige & looks, as Cup all does now, so the next one is always something to look forward to.

What I'd build next

  • P1 Replant all, P3 Night shift and P4 Old friends. All three are small: one button, one number and one list change. Each removes a chore the sims show players hitting.
  • P5 Lookout once the first three are in, since it changes how golden bushes spawn and needs a quick sim check.
  • P6 to P8 when players start reaching those prestiges after launch.
