How it's made

Nothing here is a recording. The browser plays the notes live through the same four voices as the original handheld: two square-wave "pulse" channels, a 4-bit wave channel and a noise channel. Volumes fade in small steps, the way the old hardware did it, and long lead notes get a delayed vibrato.

Pulse 1
The melody, 50% duty by day and a thinner 12.5% at night.
Pulse 2
Broken-chord arpeggios under the tune.
Wave
A walking bass from a hand-drawn 32-step waveform.
Noise
Soft brushes on the off-beats. Night drops it to a hush.
Form
Intro, then A, A′, B and A on repeat (about 1 min 25 s by day).
Size
The whole score is a few kilobytes of text, so it costs the app almost nothing to ship.
